# Business Frog
[Play Now!](https://sweetfreak.itch.io/business-frog)

Business Frog is a challenging 2D Platformer about a frog trying to get to work on time, built in Unity.

## Built With Unity
- Update to Unity 6 is underway!

## Get started
Business Frog can played on Itch.io with the link above.

## Features

Business Frog features 5 levels of increasing difficulty. Business Frog has no attacks, he can only hop. Throughout his journey, he's find enemies to bounce off of that will give him temporary special moves, such as a double jump and a dash. Can you beat the game in 10 minutes or less (and subsequently, get Business Frog to work on time)?

This game can be played with keyboard or by connecting a controller to your computer. No touch controls... yet!
